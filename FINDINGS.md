# Neural-memory depth: current findings

13 September 2026. Literature/configuration synthesis, source-loaded CPU gradient audits, five bounded synthetic recall comparisons and six frozen-readout probes. No language-model pretraining or paper reproduction.

**The published depth trends remain conditional on incompletely aligned configurations. In our synthetic task, initial representations and their interaction with the training stream strongly affect whether deeper memory reaches full or partial retrieval. A verified derivative omission matters to some trajectories, but neither correcting it nor increasing gradient-refresh frequency supplies a uniform remedy. These local findings do not establish the cause of either paper's depth curve.**

## What the sources establish

Titans v1 §5.5 tests memory without attention. Modular TTT v1 Appendix 9.4's negative depth result survives Gaussian initialization, normalization/stability variants and its 128–512 chunk sweep. Hybrid attention and the all-zero initialization trap do not resolve the contrast. Titans improves over depth 1 in its reported setting, but the gain is not monotonic at every added layer. [Initial comparison](notes/INITIAL_COMPARISON.md).

The operating points differ materially. The later Titans conference paper gives a general 16-token update chunk and a 4×-expanded GELU memory with residual and LayerNorm. Modular TTT's inspected depth configs use different graphs and mostly 256-token chunks. Its scalar write controls are already per fast node and head. Exact Titans depth-run mapping remains missing: the 16-token value is a general later-paper setting, not a verified Figure 7 configuration. Parameter and compute matching remain distinct, incompletely documented comparisons. [Configuration follow-up](notes/CONFIGURATION_FOLLOWUP.md).

The pinned Modular TTT linear error-transport primitive omits a fast-weight derivative. Source-loaded graph checks, with explicit CPU kernel substitutions, reproduce the omission in all eight deep cases while eight shallow controls match an independent reference. Numerical forward outputs/states agree, and completing the derivative agrees with finite differences. Intent and original-checkpoint provenance remain unknown; this is not proof of an unintended bug or of a paper-result error. [Primitive audit](notes/OUTER_GRADIENT_AUDIT.md), [graph audit](notes/GRAPH_EXECUTION_AUDIT.md).

## What the controlled local experiments establish

**Derivative completion and more frequent refresh do not reliably rescue deep recall under the fixed recipe.** The initial three-seed pilot found no consistent final benefit from completing the derivative. The ten-seed chunk comparison retained a much larger variation between seeds: the same seven coupled initialization/data seeds succeeded under every setting, and the same three did not. The primary chunk-1-versus-chunk-8 depth interaction had the opposite mean sign from the proposed faster-refresh benefit, was dominated by one seed, and had a descriptive interval including zero. Individual derivative effects can still be sizeable. [Pilot](notes/RECALL_PILOT_RESULTS.md), [refresh comparison](notes/REFRESH_COMPARISON_RESULTS.md).

**Separating initialization and training stream reveals substantial interaction.** In the full 10×10 crossing, initialization accounts for 40.43% of final-CE variation under the released derivative, stream for 4.59%, and their interaction for 54.98%; complete-derivative shares are 37.87%, 6.27% and 55.86%. These are descriptive sums-of-squares shares for a finite grid, not population causal-variance estimates. Three starts succeed on every stream and one on none, while several change status. “Stream” includes sampling and ordering; “initialization” includes all initial parameter groups. [Seed separation](notes/SEED_SEPARATION_RESULTS.md).

**Reciprocal tensor swaps localize strong effects to representation compatibility.** Using selected contrasting untrained donors 11 and 16, every full-group configuration with donor-11 embeddings succeeds on all ten streams under both derivatives. An embeddings-only replacement changes the difficult background from 0/10 to 10/10 low-loss outcomes. Memory-matrix replacement alone is not a reliable rescue; readout replacement alone reaches 7/10. This is an intervention on particular tensors, not evidence that one donor is universally better. [Full group swaps](notes/GROUP_SWAP_RESULTS.md).

The query/key/value factorial makes the interaction sharper. In the fixed donor-16 memory/readout background, either query-only or value-only replacement rescues all ten streams. Combining those two replacements while retaining donor-16 keys gives only 1/10 success; replacing all embeddings restores 10/10. Initial query–key cosine alignment alone cannot explain this, because changing value tensors can reverse the outcome while leaving query–key geometry unchanged. [Embedding swaps](notes/EMBEDDING_SWAP_RESULTS.md), [initial geometry](analysis/initial_geometry.json).

## What the failed checkpoints reveal

A post-hoc inspection distinguishes partial-retrieval patterns: 29 of 52 failed embedding-swap outcomes fit confusion among fixed value classes, and 7 fit confusion among fixed query keys under exploratory tolerances. Sixteen remain unclassified by these simple single-cluster descriptions. The characteristic CE levels often follow `(m/8) ln(m)` for a remaining ambiguity among m classes or episode-specific values. Per-class/per-key predictions, not the scalar loss alone, support these descriptions. [Retrieval-mode inspection](notes/RETRIEVAL_MODES.md).

Refitting a linear head on frozen normalized memory features does not resolve selected examples of either pattern. The value example remains near 7/8·ln(7), with seven unresolved values; the key example retains its two unresolved keys near the 2/8·ln(2) limit. Successful controls remain at 100% accuracy. All non-head parameters are unchanged, and every probe metric is reproduced. A regularized linear probe cannot prove absence of faint or nonlinear information, but these examples are not repaired by simply fitting a fresh linear decoder to their final features. [Frozen-readout probe](notes/FROZEN_READOUT_RESULTS.md).

Readout initialization can influence joint learning even when readout refitting cannot repair its endpoint. The supported description is **initialization- and stream-dependent partial-retrieval solutions at a fixed training horizon**. We have not established stable attractors, permanent information loss, or inability to recover through longer training. The same architecture achieves full recall under other tested starts, so these failures are not an unavoidable capacity limit on this task.

## Scope, verification and remaining uncertainty

The first intended written comparison and the subsequent bounded mechanism investigation are published locally. The empirical chain includes 592 distinct recurrent-model training runs and six additional frozen-readout fits; reused endpoints are marked and excluded from new compute counts. Saved-checkpoint metrics, source/protocol hashes, initial tensor origins and stream isolation have been verified throughout. The source checkout remains unchanged.

The task is synthetic, models and states tiny, and later interventions use adaptively selected donors and previously inspected held-out mappings. Earlier cross-depth comparisons are not parameter-matched; within-depth interventions are. Fixed training events are not compute matching, and the readout probe has a distinct data/optimization budget. CPU kernel substitutions do not establish fused CUDA/BF16 parity. Generality across donors/tasks, Titans' exact depth configurations, source-to-original-checkpoint mapping and effects on published language-model loss remain unresolved.

The findings narrow the study's question to when additional fast factors can be exploited under specified inner updates, learned representations and outer differentiation. They do not show that either paper is incorrect or that the reported tension has one universal cause. [Source ledger](notes/SOURCES.md) and the linked protocols/results preserve provenance and limits. No remote model installation was required.
